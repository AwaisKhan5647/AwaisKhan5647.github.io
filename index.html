About

I'm a PhD researcher in Computing at the University of Michigan–Flint, working on interpretable and generalizable machine learning for audio deepfake detection and multimedia forensics.

My work sits at the intersection of signal processing and deep learning, building models that don't just classify real from fake, but explain why, and that hold up when audio is compressed, re-recorded, or pushed through a platform's codec on its way to a phone screen. I work hands-on with Python and PyTorch across feature engineering, model design, and large-scale evaluation. Alongside my PhD, I work as an AI Developer at ProbeTruth, applying detection research to real-world forensic and fraud-prevention systems.
