Projects

Three connected research threads, each feeding the others — detection methods need benchmarks to prove out against, and both need to be interpretable to be trusted in forensic and legal settings.

01

Audio Deepfake Detection

Core detection research spanning spectro-temporal modeling, transformer and neuro-symbolic architectures, and robustness to real-world degradation — social media compression, codec artifacts, and re-recording. Includes SpoTNet, PSA-Net, and the ICASSP/INTERSPEECH detection lines.

PyTorch Librosa Transformers XLSR
02

Audio Deepfake Benchmarking

Adversarial-robustness benchmarks and comparative studies for existing detectors, evaluating generalization across spoofing methods, datasets, and attack types — surfacing where published detectors quietly fail outside their training distribution.

Adversarial Attacks ASVspoof Evaluation
03

Interpretable Audio Deepfake Detection

Moving detection from black-box classification toward explainable, symbolic reasoning — joint spectro-temporal and neuro-symbolic learning that produces decisions a forensic examiner or courtroom can actually audit, not just a confidence score.

Neuro-symbolic AI Explainability Forensics
